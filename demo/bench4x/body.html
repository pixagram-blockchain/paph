02The stage-1 screen

§A4 — pools only, no verification, no verdict

At index scale most pairs are not worth a full comparison, so the screen offers a cheap one that asks one cheap question: could these possibly share geometry? It counts correspondences and stops. A pair it rejects is unscreened — not unrelated, and not a verdict. That distinction is the whole reason the screen is safe to put in front of an index.

04Geometry

§12.3 + §A1 — models, and the weak signal beneath them

Where the inliers landed

Coverage over the profile's grid. One bright cell is a lifted element; a spread is a whole-work copy. This is what R3 and R4 read when they decide whether geometry may certify alone.

The weak signal

When the extraction accepts no model, comparator 42 still reports the best single verified model's inlier count — member floor kept, acceptance floor absent — and every member of the null family is measured the same way. It moves the margin. It can never certify alone: with no accepted model the topology stays scattered and R3 refuses.

05Channels, before and after their tables

§A3 — container 2 gives every channel its own calibration

06The calibration artefact

320 bytes, one SHA-256 identity, cited by every verdict

A profile is the entire judgement surface in one immutable file. Nine monotone tables in canonical order, the thresholds, the weights, the guards. Two channels carry the corpus's real knowledge: runs and palette agree strongly on almost any two pixel-art works, so their tables squash the bottom of the range hard, while geometry lifts its low band because a handful of verified inliers is worth more than a high palette score.

09Cost

§13 — budgets, and what was actually spent
Runs entirely in this page. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is fetched — no network at all, fonts included. Two engines are inlined: the Rust reference compiled to WebAssembly (SIMD128, used whenever the browser has it), and the JavaScript one — paphWire (the v3 wire) and paphjs (comparator 42, with 41 frozen beside it), from the shipped package files. They agree byte for byte, on wires and on reports; section 09 checks the second claim on every pair you compare.

Three limits, stated rather than hidden. CAL-003 is proposed, not blessed — its thresholds and all nine tables come from a 32-work development corpus whose labels were inferred by their author, not from moderation reports. A shared asset looks exactly like a partial copy: two unrelated works that embed the same licensed tile produce real, verified geometry, and no threshold separates that from a genuine crop, because the difference is provenance and provenance is not in the pixels. And a colour-inverted copy is still carried by the structural channels only: inverting flips every descriptor bit and rotates the orientation 180°, so the steered pattern samples the other side of the keypoint — measured mean Hamming distance to an inverted twin's complement is 129.9 of 256, which is chance.
